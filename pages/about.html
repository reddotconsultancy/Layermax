About Us

Message from our Director

Sojan Raju

Director, Layermax Xtended

From humble beginnings as a trading company, Layermax has evolved into a leading name in bespoke furniture. We blend modern technology with timeless design to create custom furniture for homes and workspaces — where comfort meets productivity.

Rooted in the Make in India initiative, our furniture is built by skilled Indian artisans using premium, locally sourced materials. Each piece reflects durability, design, and attention to detail — whether it’s an office chair, sofa, or workstation.

What sets us apart:

  • Tailored design-to-delivery model
  • Smart, sustainable manufacturing
  • In-house R&D for ongoing innovation
  • Proven expertise in large-scale corporate projects

At Layermax, we go beyond furniture — we craft stories of innovation and spaces that inspire. With a growing national footprint, we aim to bring world-class quality and customization to more homes and offices across India and beyond.

Premium designer chairs and comfort recliners crafted for contemporary homes and professional work environments.
